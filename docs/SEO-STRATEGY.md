# AYK Solutions — International SEO Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document defines the organic search strategy for **AYK Solutions** (`ayksolutions.com`).
The objective is to capture qualified organic traffic and generate leads across primary target markets without resorting to black-hat tactics, fake local claims, or mass-generated thin content.

## Target Markets & Hierarchy

1. **Belgium (Priority Market 1)**
   - Target languages: English (`/be/en/`), French (`/be/fr/`), Dutch (`/be/nl/`)
   - Positioning: Official business contact address, local market understanding, responsive multi-lingual web design.

2. **Saudi Arabia (Priority Market 2)**
   - Target languages: English (`/sa/en/`), Arabic (`/sa/ar/`)
   - Positioning: Bilingual English–Arabic web development, RTL design expertise, ecommerce, regional business suitability.

3. **Australia (Priority Market 3)**
   - Target languages: English (`/au/en/`)
   - Positioning: Remote web design partner for Australian small businesses, tradies, consultants, and service providers.

4. **Europe & Global (Secondary)**
   - Target language: English (`/`)
   - Positioning: Quality small-business websites, web applications, and custom software launched fast.

## Core Rules & Ethics

- **No misleading claims:** Do not claim a physical storefront or local staff unless true. Address is labeled as "Belgium Business Contact".
- **No thin content:** Every regional page must contain unique, market-relevant information, FAQs, and industry alignment.
- **No IP auto-redirects:** Users and search crawlers must be free to navigate any regional URL.
- **No invented statistics:** All stats used on the website must be verifiable.

## Architecture

- Subdirectory structure: `/be/`, `/sa/`, `/au/`
- Standardized metadata templates with `metadataBase = new URL("https://ayksolutions.com")`
- Complete bi-directional `hreflang` alternate links
- JSON-LD structured data (Organization, WebSite, WebPage, Service, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage)
